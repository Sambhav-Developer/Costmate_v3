PROJECT SCOPE:
The scope of the project is the renovation of several areas of the property identified on the drawing package or packages issued with this specification.
Project: Northeast Medical Group (YNHH) Ambulatory Expansion, 440 Mamaroneck Ave, Harrison, NY (Suite 301). The project is a renovation and expansion of an outpatient medical clinic on the 3rd floor. 
Key Observations
Existing healthcare facility remains operational during construction; phasing, infection control, and life-safety measures are critical. 
Occupancy classification is Group B (Business) with an occupant load of 81 persons on the project floor and total exit capacity of 393 persons. 
Designed in compliance with 2025 NY State codes, ADA, NFPA 101, and FGI healthcare guidelines. 
Significant demolition includes removal of partitions, doors, ceilings, flooring, glazing, and casework before reconstruction. 
Strong emphasis on accessibility, fire protection, infection control, and maintaining hospital operations during renovation.

Overall Assessment
The project is a comprehensive outpatient clinic renovation and expansion focused on increasing exam capacity, improving staff workflow, modernizing MEP systems, improving patient experience, and ensuring full ADA, healthcare, and life-safety compliance. Key risks and coordination points are phased demolition, infection control, access-control integration, sprinkler/fire alarm modifications, and maintaining clinic operations during construction.
